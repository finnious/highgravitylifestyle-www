Gulf Condo Week

September 21–27, 2018

Late August, Max Withdrawal locked a Gulf window: drive to Orange Beach Friday September 21 after two, or Saturday morning if the day ran long. Home to Memphis Thursday the 27th. Partner circle, not the crew.

The plan was to split nights between the condo and a friend’s house. The condo came open. The friend’s house was already good for the whole stay if it hadn’t. Mid-September the itinerary went out lived: Friday arrive around midnight, Saturday dinner with home, Wednesday dinner with home again, Monday open for friends on the beach, Thursday wheels north.

What filled the open Sundays and Tuesdays isn’t in the thread. The drive dates and the condo are.

The Gulf was the week. The itinerary mail was the receipt.
