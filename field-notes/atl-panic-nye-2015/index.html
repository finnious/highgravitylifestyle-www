Airbnb Wouldn’t Take Him

December 29, 2015 – January 3, 2016

December 28, subject line plain: ATL this week. Max Withdrawal told the crew he was coming down for the Widespread Panic shows on the 29th, 30th, and 31st. Drew was already in town. Max figured he’d stay through about January 2 or 3.

He found what he called the perfect post-show Airbnb.

they only take female guest :(

A friend offered a bedroom instead — accepts males, friends-and-family discount. Someone floated group lunch during the Auburn game. Ticketmaster never landed in this inbox for the three nights. No thanks-for-attending either. The hour the mail proves is the in-town window and the listing that wouldn’t take him.

The shows were the reason to go. The Airbnb was the joke. The bedroom was the backup.
