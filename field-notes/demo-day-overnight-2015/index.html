Overnight for Demo Day

February 11–12, 2015

January 17. Wednesday February 11 Memphis 11:30pm into Atlanta 8:00am. Thursday the 12th back out at 11:45pm, Memphis 6:15am Friday. Forty-three fifty. Same day he mailed a short note: Atlanta Tech Demo Night on the 12th — all he knew was the webpage, but he was going.

Morning of the eleventh he told a contact he was headed to Atlanta for a Demo Day event. Afternoon clarification: Megabus overnight down tonight, overnight back tomorrow night, Memphis around six Friday morning. Friday the thirteenth he was home and asking to meet after lunch.

The overnight was the whole Atlanta window. In by eight, out by midnight, home for Friday.

The tech night was the reason. The double overnight was the shape of the day.
