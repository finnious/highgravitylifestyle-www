Disc Golf at Mountain Sun

December 28, 2011. Mountain Sun, in Boulder. A high gravity travel update that barely left the room.

Two groups, separate from each other, said "disc golf" loud enough to overhear.

No score. No course. The word, twice, in a bar where the conversation was already going. Worth writing down.
