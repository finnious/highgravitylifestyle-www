Dollar Seats to Alpharetta

July 16–18, 2013

April 5. Tuesday July 16 Memphis 7:00am into Civic Center at 3:55pm. Thursday the 18th home at 1:30pm. One dollar each way. Fifty-cent booking fee. Thirty cents for a text Max called very sneaky. Total: two eighty. Subject line to the crew: Megabus ATL PHiSH trip. Drew’s plan was Marta to the office unless somebody wanted to drive.

March had already bought the Alpharetta nights. Tuesday lawn and Wednesday orchestra at Encore Park, two each, on the Musictoday receipt. Dick’s in Colorado rode the same receipt; that is a different week.

Attendance mail for the Alpharetta shows is not in this pass. The locked hour is the dollar seat and the Marta handoff.

The text fee was thirty cents. The ride was a dollar. Drew’s office was the first stop.
