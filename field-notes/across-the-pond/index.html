Across the Pond

September 3, 2009. High Gravity Travel, before it had much of a log. Friends heading across the pond. Andrew locked the seats.

Just locked in some exit row seats for us for our trip across the pond #twerk #Delta #SilverMedallion

First row of the section, near the bulkhead. Maybe nothing in front. Restrooms close, which he treated as a feature.

There should be plenty of room for flexing... I think we are in the first row of the section near the bulk head, so we might not have any seats in front of us... we should be right near the restrooms too which will make our scotch drinking and mile-high opportunities with any onboard hotties more convenient.

Then the exit row as a bit of theater.

we should have enough room in our exit row to do some rocking so they think we're about to head out...

No city list came with it. The pond, the row, the scotch. That was the plan on paper.
