Across the Pond

September 3, 2009

Friends were heading across the pond. Andrew locked exit-row seats on Delta and wrote the update like the seats were the trip. No cities. The row was the plan.

There should be plenty of room for flexing... I think we are in the first row of the section near the bulk head, so we might not have any seats in front of us... we should be right near the restrooms too which will make our scotch drinking and mile-high opportunities with any onboard hotties more convenient.

He had a second use for the space. Enough room in that row, he said, to do some rocking, so the cabin might think they were about to head out.

Across the pond, with friends, in seats picked for the joke as much as the flight. That was the itinerary.
