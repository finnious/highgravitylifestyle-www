Across the Pond

September 3, 2009

Friends were heading across the pond. In August the note was cities. On September 3 it was the row.

London through Rob and Sarah, and a pint when they met. St Christopher's for the hostel. Then the rail. A cheap flight from Paris to Amsterdam would have dropped Belgium.

The tips that came back were Whitestone's mom's. First class on the train from London to Paris was worth it. Prague was the favorite city on that list, second to Venice. Drew answered with times and skipped the flight. The euro rail pass was first class. Keeping the trains kept Bruges.

  • London to Paris, 2 hours 25 minutes
  • Paris to Brussels, 1 hour 35 minutes
  • Brussels to Bruges, 1 hour 2 minutes
  • Bruges to Amsterdam, 3 hours 32 minutes
  • Amsterdam to Prague, overnight, 14 hours 15 minutes

London, Paris, Brussels, Bruges, Amsterdam, Prague. That was the shape. Italy, still in handwriting, is the next page.

Drew locked exit-row seats on Delta and wrote that update like the row still had a job. It did. It was no longer the whole plan.

There should be plenty of room for flexing... I think we are in the first row of the section near the bulk head, so we might not have any seats in front of us... we should be right near the restrooms too.

Close to the restrooms, which made the scotch easier. He had a second use for the space. Enough room in that row, he said, to do some rocking, so the cabin might think they were about to head out.

Across the pond, with friends. Cities on the rail pass. Seats picked for the joke as much as the flight.
