State of the Gravity — 2009

This is the first one. Every January after, a note on the year and what got packed into it. The series starts in 2009 and runs from there.

January 4, 2009 is the anchor. Two friends, high-gravity beers, a domain bought as a goof. Living full, not loud. The about page tells that night. Where It Started is the short version. This post is the hook those pages hang on: look back, once a year, at what the year actually held.

In the log, 2009 holds two things. That night. And a September trip across the pond. Exit-row seats. Friends traveling. Scotch, legroom, and a joke written down with the tags still on it.

Not a crowded year on paper. Enough to start the habit.
