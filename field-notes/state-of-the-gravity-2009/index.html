State of the Gravity — 2009

Looking back at 2009

January 4, 2009. Two friends, high-gravity beers, and a domain bought as a goof. The year opened on that joke. Living full, not loud.

The other thing 2009 holds is September. Friends across the pond. An exit row near the bulkhead. Scotch close to the restrooms. A plan to rock in the seats until the cabin got the wrong idea.

A short list. The night the name showed up, and the flight that carried it out of the bar. That is what got packed into the year.
