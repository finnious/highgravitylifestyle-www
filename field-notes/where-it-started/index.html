Field Notes #1 — Where It Started

January 4th, 2009. Two friends, too many high-gravity beers, and a bad economy. We made a joke about living a high-gravity lifestyle — living full, not loud. Bought the domain that night. Forgot about it for years. Pulled it back out when the idea finally made sense. This is the first note. More to come.

More life per hour.
