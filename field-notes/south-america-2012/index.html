Atlanta, Buenos Aires, Lima

February 29, 2012

The 2012 route got written down at the edges and left open in the middle.

September 28, Atlanta to Buenos Aires. October 14, Lima back to Atlanta. Delta on both. A hop through Peru was still to be decided, and the note did not dress that up as a plan.

Buenos Aires in. Lima out. More than two weeks between the doors, real on the calendar, unfinished on the ticket. The gap is part of the story. It was never filled in on paper.
