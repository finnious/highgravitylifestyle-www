Atlanta, Buenos Aires, Lima

February 29, 2012

February 29, 2012. BJ Quick got the doors, Drew on the copy. Delta 101, Atlanta at 9:00 p.m. on September 28, Buenos Aires at 8:10 the next morning. Delta 150, Lima at 12:40 a.m. on October 14, Atlanta at 8:30 a.m. The hop to Peru was still open.

August 29, Drew sent Max Withdrawal the days.

  • Fri Sep 28: Delta 101, Atlanta 9:00 p.m.
  • Sat Sep 29: Buenos Aires 8:10 a.m. Palermo, Soler 4860, apartment 303, through Oct 3. Host Savas. Code M93MFK.
  • Sun Sep 30 – Tue Oct 2: Buenos Aires. Same apartment.
  • Wed Oct 3: Andesmar Chevallier, Retiro to Mendoza, 8:50 p.m. Top deck. Max and Drew in the windshield seats.
  • Thu Oct 4: Mendoza 9:55 a.m.
  • Fri Oct 5: Mendoza.
  • Sat Oct 6: 5:15 p.m. LAN933 / LAN642 / LAN2025. Mendoza to Cuzco. Santiago and Lima only to change planes.
  • Sun Oct 7: Cuzco 6:00 a.m. Hotel Torre Dorada, code SF07-10-12HY, through Oct 10, and again the night of Oct 12.
  • Mon Oct 8 – Wed Oct 10: Cuzco. Torre Dorada.
  • Thu Oct 11: Early train to Ollantaytambo. Inca Trail, day 1. Night in Aguas Calientes. Bags held at Torre Dorada.
  • Fri Oct 12: Inca Trail, day 2. Huayna Picchu. Train back to Cuzco, and Torre Dorada.
  • Sat Oct 13: TACA12, Cuzco 1:15 p.m., Lima 2:40 p.m.
  • Sun Oct 14: Delta 150, Lima 12:40 a.m., Atlanta 8:30 a.m.

A PayPal split paid to Drew covered the Mendoza–Cuzco flight, the Machu Picchu excursion with the Aguas Calientes night, and Cuzco–Lima. Mendoza never got a hotel name.
