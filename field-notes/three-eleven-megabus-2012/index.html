Three Dollars Overnight for 311

July 20–22, 2012

May 18, Max Withdrawal booked the overnight and forwarded it to the crew in one line.

311 weekend travel booked for $2.80

Friday July 20, Memphis MATA at 4:00pm. Atlanta MARTA Civic Center at 12:55am Saturday. Home Sunday at 1:30pm. One dollar each way, eighty cents in fees. A friend already had the Lakewood tickets for Saturday the 21st — 311 and Slightly Stoopid at Aaron’s Amphitheatre. Drew promised a spot right in front of SA when the bus rolled in.

The mailbox locks the overnight and the label. It does not lock a Scott Ticketmaster order or a thanks-for-attending. The hour that is certain is the seat that cost less than a coffee.

The friend got the tickets. The two-eighty got Max to the city.
