Good Vibes at the Tabernacle

September 14, 2013

August 20, on Live Nation. Two general admission for Rebelution and Matisyahu — The Good Vibes Summer Tour — at the Tabernacle, Saturday September 14, seven o’clock. Eighty dollars for the pair. Collie Buddz and Zion I on the same bill. Max Withdrawal bought them the same afternoon he was stacking Megabus seats for other Atlanta weekends.

The show was Saturday. Live Nation mailed the thanks on the seventeenth. You went. You watched. The Tabernacle held another Atlanta night.

No overnight reservation for that weekend showed up in this pass. The objects that did: the eighty-dollar order and the post-show mail.

The tour name was Good Vibes. The receipt was two general admission and a thanks.
