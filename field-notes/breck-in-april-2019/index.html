Breck After the Date Change

April 10–14, 2019

This one was Max Withdrawal’s bachelor week — Denver to Breckenridge, crew-sized house, someone on lodging, someone on the grill plan, Drew on the hangouts. First dates were April 3–8. Then the mail went out: dates change. New window April 10–14. Headcount needed so a friend could book.

By the week of, flights were real. A minivan was leaving DEN for Breck Wednesday around 2pm. Max’s plan: maybe rent a snowboard Thursday, into Breck Wednesday with the van, back toward Denver Sunday with a full car, DEN departure Monday. Someone else priced a one-way Summit Express shuttle at $44. Return home locked on Frontier — DEN 1:40pm to MEM Monday, April 15, seventy-four dollars with a checked bag.

Eight Airbnb and VRBO options had been shortlisted for fifteen to twenty people. Which house they actually slept in isn’t in the thread.

The date change cut some of the invite list. The ones who could still make April showed up for the mountain anyway.
