Vegas on Miles

October 6, 2010

A friend in Vegas, in December. That was the pull. Andrew booked the flight and wrote the group as if the fare were the funny part.

I just booked a flight to Vegas for Dec... Got exit row both ways (the further back of the two seats so I can still put my seat down - Captain Smith recommends this). Did Delta 'Pay with Miles' - total cost $7.40 + 35,000 miles #HGTCo #Twerk

A seat that still reclines. Miles doing almost all of the paying. The friend was the reason to go, and the number at the bottom was the reason it was easy to say yes.
