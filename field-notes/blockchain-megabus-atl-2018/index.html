Blockchain Overnight

June 26–27, 2018

June 19. Tuesday June 26 Memphis American Way at 11:45pm into Civic Center at 8:20am. Wednesday the 27th Atlanta 11:50pm home at 6:20am. Seat four both ways. Fifty-two fifty. The calendar block read MegaBus ATL Blockchain Trip — Tuesday 11pm through Thursday morning.

Afternoon of the twenty-sixth Max told a riding friend he was going to Atlanta for a Blockchain meetup tomorrow night, week packed with meetings. The meetup room and the deck never made it into a follow-up thread.

Overnight down, one Atlanta day, overnight home. Business density on a bus seat.

The calendar named the trip. The fare was fifty-two fifty. The middle of the day stayed off the mail.
