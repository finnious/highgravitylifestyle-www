BAW III — ATL Strikes Back

April 26, 2011

A year earlier someone had pitched BAW III: Vegas. Save the miles. Don’t chicken out because it needs a plane ticket.

April 26, 2011, Drew sent the real one. Subject: BAW III — ATL Strikes Back. No body. One photo.

Through a doorway: folding table, red cups in triangles at both ends, a glass boot standing in the middle with a stuffed monkey in the mouth. On the wall behind it, a black banner in carnival letters.

GET YOUR BALLS WET
BEER PONG

Same line as the night before BAW II. Different year. Still Atlanta.

The Vegas plan stayed a plan. The table was the proof.
