Italy Notes

September 5, 2009

Two days after the exit row, Drew sent the Italy notes. A photo of handwriting, not a typed list. The page would not resolve. One line did.

Hasselhof charges Finney.

That was the line worth keeping. The rest stayed on the paper.

October 19, 2011, Drew wrote the memory the photo never held. The train back to Milan with Finney. Giant bowls of fried rice from the Florence station. Finney singing "Bah dap ba..." while Drew was trying to sleep. He almost smacked him for it.

Handwriting in 2009. The train, told in 2011. Florence to Milan is what survived.
