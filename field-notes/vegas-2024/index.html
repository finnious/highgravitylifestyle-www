Thursday Out, Saturday Back

March 7, 2024

January 29, 2024. Max Withdrawal asked at home for Thursday out and Saturday back. A friend's fiftieth, in Vegas.

The days sat at the tail of a Hawaii trip. The calendar was clear. Home said that was fine.

The next day Spirit confirmed.

  • Thu Mar 7: Spirit 3373, Memphis 11:48 a.m., Las Vegas 1:47 p.m.
  • Sat Mar 9: Spirit 3374, Las Vegas 12:26 p.m., Memphis 5:40 p.m.

The birthday was the reason to go. The room never got a name.
