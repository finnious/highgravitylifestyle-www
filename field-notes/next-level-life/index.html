Next Level Life

February 17, 2014

Float Conference dates were going around. Post-Float Coffees came up with them, a marketing joke more than a drink. A float-tank owner with a business mind could make money at that. Not the kind trying to live on a commune.

From there the bit became a product. High Gravity Travel, the note claimed, could organize your transition to a Next Level Life. The packages read like a brochure from friends who should not be trusted with a printer.

  • Float Tank
  • TM training
  • Tripping camp
  • Disc golf training
  • Megabus tips/tricks
  • coffee brewing

Drew answered with the free gift.

I think we could throw in a free cane & a couple of bags of pistachios for the first group of customers.

A company, for the length of the thread. Float, disc golf, the bus, the coffee, and the camps that keep it from sounding legitimate. The cane and the pistachios closed it. Nobody was actually selling.
