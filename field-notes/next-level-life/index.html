Next Level Life

February 17, 2014. Float Conference dates got passed along. Post-Float Coffees came up, as a marketing joke. A float-tank owner with a business mind could make money. Not the live-on-a-commune type.

Then a package list, written in full.

High gravity travel can organize your transition to living a Next Level Life

Packages include:

  • Float Tank
  • TM training
  • Tripping camp
  • Next Level Tripping camp (includes throw up ceremony)
  • Disc golf training
  • weed travel instruction
  • Megabus tips/tricks
  • coffee brewing

Andrew wrote back:

I think we could throw in a free cane & a couple of bags of pistachios for the first group of customers.

It reads like a menu and a parody of one. Float tanks, disc golf, Megabus, coffee. The camps and the weed line stayed in because that was the bit. Not a brochure. A note between friends.
