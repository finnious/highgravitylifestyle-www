Pork Knuckle

November 24, 2009

A week before Prague, the brief was already on the phone. Max Withdrawal to Drew: Czechs drink more beer per capita than any other people. Spelling optional. Sent from the iPhone.

Drew came back with the other half of the table.

If u watched #threesheets you'd also know how much they love #porkknucle

Three Sheets had already done the Czech Republic. The knuckle was the punchline sitting next to the beer fact. No restaurant named. No receipt. The trip still had a week to board.

Prague was the city. The hour was the menu arriving before the train.
