Chuckles Weekend

June 11–15, 2015

June 5, Max asked Drew whether to arrive Friday morning or Saturday for Comedy weekend. Either works, Drew said — lots of coffee. That evening the booking landed: Thursday June 11 Memphis 11:59pm into Atlanta 8:55am, seat two reserved. Monday the 15th home at 11:50pm. Sixty-one fifty. Forwarded with the label: booked my Comedy weekend trip.

Drew’s reply was three words of hashtag: #Chuckles.

By the tenth a gathering mail was out for Drew’s big show — career-launch celebrations, strong crowd expected. Max wrote that he’d let all his new Megabus friends know. The set list and the club name are not in Scott’s confirmation trail. The bus and the Chuckles tag are.

The weekend was comedy. The receipt was a reserved overnight seat and a hashtag.
