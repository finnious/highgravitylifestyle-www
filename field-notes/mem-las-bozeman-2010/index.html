Memphis, Las Vegas, Bozeman

November 4, 2010. The rest of the year, written down as a loop.

  • December 1: Memphis to Las Vegas.
  • December 4: Las Vegas to Bozeman.
  • December 11: Bozeman to Memphis.

Delta on every leg. Out through the desert, up to Montana, home again.

The Las Vegas days were the December trip already booked. Bozeman was the turn. The note did not say what Montana was for. Cities and dates were the whole itinerary.
