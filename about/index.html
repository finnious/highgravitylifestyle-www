About

On January 4th, 2009, two friends were drinking high-gravity beers and made a joke about living a high-gravity lifestyle — living full, not loud. We bought the domain that night as a goof.

Seventeen years later, the joke still holds. High Gravity Lifestyle is about packing more life into the same hours. Not extreme sports. Not stunts. Just choosing people, places, and moments that matter, and refusing to let them pass by unnoticed.

This site is a work in progress. We're building it in public, one field note at a time.
