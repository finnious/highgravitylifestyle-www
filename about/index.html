About

On January 4, 2009, two friends sat with high-gravity beers and named what they already wanted: a high-gravity life. Full, not loud. The domain was bought before last call.

Seventeen years on, the phrase still holds. Pack more life into the same hours. Not a stunt. A habit of choosing, then showing up.

One books the exit row. One keeps the handwriting. The hours are shared either way.

High Gravity Lifestyle is the compass. High Gravity Travel Co is the vehicle. One tells you what belongs in the hour. The other gets you there.

The Field Notes are the proof — 2009 through 2024, oldest first.

More life per hour.
